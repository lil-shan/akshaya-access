**N1 · Minor | backend | `app.py:368`.** `secrets.choice` over the free 4-digit numbers raises `IndexError` → HTTP 500 if all 9000 case numbers are queued. Demo-irrelevant; fix: on exhaustion reuse a `case_id` suffix or return a guard error.

**N2 · Minor | voice | `sim.py:106-108`.** A non-numeric `PAYMENT_POLL_INTERVAL_S`/`PAYMENT_POLL_TIMEOUT_S` crashes with a `ValueError` traceback before the call. Fix: wrap the `float()` in argparse/`try` and exit with a one-line message. Engine-side validation itself is correct.

**N3 · Info | backend | `app.py:129-132`.** The redactor covers digit runs only; a client posting `citizen_said` "my pin is four eight two six" stores it verbatim. The contract's rule is digit-runs-only and voice never posts raw speech, so this is a contract-accepted residual — keep it in mind if other producers ever write `citizen_said`.

**N4 · Info | voice | `sim.py:64-72`, `sarvam.py:28-31`.** Audio mode still sends raw citizen audio to Sarvam STT before any classification — unavoidable for remote STT. Mitigations now in place (mic open only during citizen turns, WAV deleted after transcribe, opening disclosure). Orchestrator should consciously accept this for the demo; the alternative was synthetic-only audio.

**N5 · Operational | backend | `backend/data.db`.** Holds stale `pay_3f767fae935a` pending on `KSEB-SIM-0001` (the demo bill). Under the new rule any start_payment for that bill → `payment_already_started` → voice hands off `payment_problem`. Before the demo, delete the DB or complete the payment via `/simulator/payments/.../complete`.
